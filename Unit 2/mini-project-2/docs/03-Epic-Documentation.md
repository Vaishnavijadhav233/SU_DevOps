# 3. Epic Documentation

## Epic 1 — Student Management
**Purpose:** Maintain student records throughout their academic lifecycle.  
**Scope:** Student registration, profile management, search, update, and deletion.  
**Expected outcome:** Authorized users can maintain accurate student information.

## Epic 2 — Course Management
**Purpose:** Maintain course information and student-course associations.  
**Scope:** Add and update courses, assign students, and view course details.  
**Expected outcome:** Course information is organized and accessible.

## Epic 3 — Attendance Management
**Purpose:** Record and review student attendance.  
**Scope:** Select a course and date, record attendance, view records, and prepare attendance reports.  
**Expected outcome:** Attendance information is stored consistently and can be reviewed.

## Epic 4 — Marks and Academic Performance
**Purpose:** Maintain assessment marks and communicate academic performance.  
**Scope:** Enter and update marks, calculate grades using approved rules, and generate result sheets.  
**Expected outcome:** Academic performance can be reviewed from reliable records.

## Epic 5 — Authentication and Security
**Purpose:** Restrict access to authorized users.  
**Scope:** Login, logout, password reset, and role-based access control.  
**Expected outcome:** Users can access only the functions permitted for their roles.

## Epic 6 — Notifications
**Purpose:** Inform relevant users about important academic events.  
**Scope:** Attendance alerts, examination notifications, and result notifications.  
**Expected outcome:** Users receive relevant and timely information.

## Epic 7 — Reporting
**Purpose:** Provide summaries for academic administration and decision-making.  
**Scope:** Student, attendance, performance, and course reports.  
**Expected outcome:** Users can review organized summaries of academic data.

## Dependencies and notes
- Authentication and authorization should protect sensitive features.
- Student and course records are prerequisites for meaningful attendance and marks records.
- Notifications depend on the events and contact preferences defined by the institution.
- Reporting depends on the availability and quality of stored data.
