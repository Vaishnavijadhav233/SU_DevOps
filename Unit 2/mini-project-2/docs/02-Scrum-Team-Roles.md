|---|---|---|
| Product Owner | [Enter name] | Defines requirements, represents stakeholders, orders the Product Backlog, and accepts completed work. |
| Scrum Master | [Enter name] | Facilitates Scrum events, helps remove impediments, and supports the team's use of Scrum. |
| Developers | [Enter names] | Design, implement, test, document, and demonstrate usable increments. |

## Team working agreements
- Keep the Product Backlog visible and prioritized.
- Discuss blockers early.
- Review acceptance criteria before marking a story complete.
- Test each increment before demonstration.
- Record decisions and update the Scrum Board after work changes.
- Use the Sprint Review for feedback and the Retrospective for process improvement.

If the project is completed individually, explain that one student is performing the responsibilities of all three roles rather than inventing team members.
