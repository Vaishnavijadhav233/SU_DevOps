# 10. Scrum Ceremonies

## Sprint Planning
**Purpose:** Agree on why the Sprint is valuable, what can be completed, and how the work will be done.  
**Activities:** Select backlog items, review dependencies, estimate effort, define a Sprint Goal, and create the Sprint Backlog.

## Daily Scrum
**Purpose:** Inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapt the plan.  
Each team member briefly answers:
1. What did I complete since the last meeting?
2. What will I work on next?
3. What is blocking my progress?

## Sprint Review
**Purpose:** Inspect the increment and collect stakeholder feedback.  
**Activities:** Demonstrate completed work, compare it with acceptance criteria, collect feedback, and update the Product Backlog.

## Sprint Retrospective
**Purpose:** Identify improvements to teamwork and the development process.  
Discuss:
- What went well?
- What problems occurred?
- What should be improved in the next Sprint?
