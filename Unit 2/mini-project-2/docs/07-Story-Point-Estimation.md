# 7. Story Point Estimation

Story points are relative estimates of effort, complexity, and uncertainty; they are not hours.

## Fibonacci-style scale used in this plan

| Points | Complexity | Typical meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Very easy | Small, well-understood change |
| 2 | Easy | Limited work and uncertainty |
| 3 | Moderate | Several straightforward steps |
| 5 | Difficult | Multiple tasks or test cases |
| 8 | Very difficult | Significant complexity or dependencies |
| 13 | Highly complex | Too large or uncertain; consider splitting |

## Estimation method
The team can use Planning Poker: each member estimates independently, the team discusses major differences, and a shared estimate is agreed. Estimates should be updated if the team's understanding changes.
