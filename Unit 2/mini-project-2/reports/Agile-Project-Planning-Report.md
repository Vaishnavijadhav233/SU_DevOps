# Agile Project Planning Report

## 1. Introduction
This report presents the Agile planning for a Student Record and Academic Management Platform. The goal is to organize the proposed system into manageable increments and define how requirements will be prioritized, estimated, delivered, and reviewed.

## 2. Business problem
Student information, course details, attendance, and academic results need to be managed accurately and accessed by authorized users. A centralized platform can reduce fragmented record keeping and make information easier to review.

## 3. Product vision
The proposed product is a secure, user-friendly web-based platform for student records, courses, attendance, academic performance, and reporting.

## 4. Users and stakeholders
Primary users include administrators, faculty members, and students. Institution management and other authorized staff may also provide requirements and feedback.

## 5. Agile approach
Scrum is selected as the main planning framework because the assignment includes Scrum roles, a Product Backlog, Sprint Planning, a Scrum Board, Sprint Reviews, and Retrospectives.

## 6. Product scope
The identified epics are Student Management, Course Management, Attendance Management, Marks and Academic Performance, Authentication and Security, Notifications, and Reporting. The initial MVP should focus on authentication, student records, courses, attendance, and basic reports.

## 7. Backlog and estimation
User stories are written from the user's perspective and supported by acceptance criteria. The backlog is prioritized by business value, user importance, dependencies, and complexity. Initial story-point estimates use a relative scale of 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, and 13.

## 8. Sprint plan
- Sprint 1: Project setup and authentication.
- Sprint 2: Student management.
- Sprint 3: Course and attendance management.

The team should revise the plan based on capacity, feedback, and actual progress.

## 9. Quality and acceptance
A backlog item is accepted only when its acceptance criteria are satisfied, relevant tests pass, and the team reviews the result. Security and data validation are important because the platform handles academic records.

## 10. Risks
Potential risks include unclear requirements, scope growth, incorrect data, unauthorized access, and limited time. The team should address these through stakeholder clarification, backlog prioritization, validation, access control, and regular inspection.

## 11. Conclusion
The Agile plan divides the proposed platform into epics, user stories, and incremental Sprints. Regular review and adaptation will help the team focus on valuable features and improve the plan as more information becomes available.

**Project status note:** This report describes planning artifacts. Update it with implementation evidence and actual Sprint results if the application is developed.
